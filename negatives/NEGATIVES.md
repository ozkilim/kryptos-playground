## statistics

- **Pure transposition, or monoalphabetic substitution followed by transposition**
  - Scope: IoC argument on the 97 carved letters
  - Outcome: Ruled out: K4 IoC 0.036 vs about 0.066 for English, so a non-transposition substitution layer is present
  - Solid elimination · gereon/kryptos, 2026-09 · [source](https://github.com/gereon/kryptos)
- **K4's 97 letters fill a complete rectangular grid with no padding or nulls**
  - Scope: Length of the carved K4 ciphertext
  - Outcome: Ruled out: 97 is prime, so any grid needs padding, ragged rows or a null
  - Solid elimination · TRIED.md compiler (checked with its own code) (via TRIED.md compilation), by 2026-11 · [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kryptos)
- **A candidate decryption may put the known plaintext at other positions**
  - Scope: Sanborn's released cribs: EASTNORTHEAST at 22-34 and BERLINCLOCK at 64-74 (24 letters, 1-indexed)
  - Outcome: Ruled out: every candidate must place these 24 letters exactly at these positions
  - Solid elimination · TRIED.md compiler; cribs released by Jim Sanborn 2010-2020 (via TRIED.md compilation), by 2026-11 · [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kryptos)
- **Pure transposition, alone or in any combination of transpositions**
  - Scope: Letter-count proof: the cribs need 3 E's but the 97-letter ciphertext has only 2
  - Outcome: Ruled out by proof (stronger than the IoC argument): some layer must change letters
  - Solid elimination · TRIED.md compiler (checked with its own code) (via TRIED.md compilation), by 2026-11 · [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kryptos)
- **Ciphers that never encrypt a letter to itself (Enigma with reflector, Porta, Playfair)**
  - Scope: Two crib fixed points: K->K at 74 and also S->S at 33 (existing entries cite only K->K)
  - Outcome: Ruled out: two independent self-encryptions confirm fixed points are allowed
  - Solid elimination · TRIED.md compiler (checked with its own code) (via TRIED.md compilation), by 2026-11 · [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kryptos)
- **Fits at key length 19 (or 38) are evidence for that period**
  - Scope: Crib pair R->P occurs twice, at 28 and 66 (38 apart); the only repeated plain/cipher pair
  - Outcome: Observation: this one repeat explains many 'lucky' fits at length 19; treat such fits as expected, not signal
  - Solid elimination · TRIED.md compiler (checked with its own code) (via TRIED.md compilation), by 2026-11 · [source](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/153/109)

  - Solid elimination · TRIED.md compiler (checked with its own code) (via TRIED.md compilation), by 2026-11 · [source](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/153/109)

  - Solid elimination · TRIED.md compiler (checked with its own code) (via TRIED.md compilation), by 2026-11 · [source](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/153/109)
- **Observation: Y and M each appear only once in K4**
  - Scope: Letter counts of the 97-letter ciphertext. TRIED.md attributes the note to Jim Gillogly. Source is the closest one listed in TRIED.md; no direct URL given for this item.
  - Outcome: Fact; no method derived from it

- **A short repeating key alone (detectable by Kasiski or Friedman tests)**
  - Scope: Kasiski and Friedman tests; entropy 4.55 bits/letter (random ceiling) vs 4.35-4.39 for K1/K2
  - Outcome: Ruled out: no period shows; if a short memorable key was used it was expanded nonlinearly
  - Solid elimination · TRIED.md compiler (checked with its own code) (via TRIED.md compilation), by 2026-11 · [source](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/153/109)
- **K4 involves transposition (tested statistically against letter-difference and width-21/width-7 regularities)**
  - Scope: Permutation tests: width-21 vertical bigram repeats (11 of 76, ~1 in 6,750); Materna minor differences (~1 in 5,520); repeated-plaintext cipher distances (p ~1/240-1/1,470)
  - Outcome: Bean argues one-to-one single-letter encryption, i.e. no transposition; tests were chosen after looking at the data, so suggestive not conclusive
  - Partial or weak test · Richard Bean, 2021 · [source](https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/153/109)
- **K4 is a single ACA cipher type**
  - Scope: Argument from low IoC (~0.036) and the 2020 clues
  - Outcome: Commenter argues a combination of two or more types is needed; opinion with partial evidence, no test
  - Partial or weak test · elahieh (Hacker News), 2025-08-15 · [source](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44907366)
- **The Ws in K4 are word or segment separators (W-delimiter hypothesis)**
  - Scope: Every simple divider version, plus a 2026 audit deleting non-W letters
  - Outcome: All divider versions failed; audit found the oddity also appears when non-W letters are deleted, so it says nothing about the Ws
  - Partial or weak test · kryptosbot.com (Colin Patrick with Claude), 2026-10-12 · [source](https://www.kryptosbot.com/)
- **Stehle's DIAWINFBN run (five equal letter differences at lag 4) is a chance artifact**
  - Scope: 200,000 shuffled K4s; statistic = longest run of constant difference over any lag 1-20 (corrects for lag choice)
  - Outcome: Not chance by this test: p=0.0032. Caveat: found post hoc among many patterns, so the true p is weaker. Points at lag-4 structure.
  - Partial or weak test · this repo (campaign idea 16), 2026-11-15 · campaign/results/16.json
- **K4's doubled letters (5 of 6 on one residue mod 7) are a chance artifact**
  - Scope: 200,000 shuffled K4s; statistic = best excess concentration over moduli 2-12 (corrects for modulus choice)
  - Outcome: Not chance by this test: p=0.0011 overall, 0.0072 among shuffles with >=6 doublets. Caveat: post hoc. Supports a period-7 / width-7 component.
  - Partial or weak test · this repo (campaign idea 16), 2026-11-15 · campaign/results/16.json
- **Observation: the 7/21 rhythm (width-21 vertical repeats, width-7 doublets)**
  - Scope: 11 repeated vertical pairs at width 21; 5 of 6 doublets align mod 7; inside the cribs every width-7 column 5->6 step is a doublet; look-elsewhere correction
  - Outcome: ~1 in 4,000 raw, ~1 in 90-210 after best-width correction, ~1 in 10 without the doublets; a hint, not a method
  - Partial or weak test · barneyjm/kryptos-k4, reggiechan74/kryptos-k4 (building on Bean) (via TRIED.md compilation), by 2026-11 · [source](https://github.com/barneyjm/kryptos-k4)
- **Observation: plaintext letters in KRYPTOS encrypt to within +/-2 of themselves**
  - Scope: 9 of 10 known cases; barneyjm re-test (https://github.com/barneyjm/kryptos-k4); first noted by Greg (r/UnresolvedMysteries 2020)
  - Outcome: Post hoc: Barney gets 1 in 4,500 for the subset, 0.14 for all letters; do not use as a hard filter
  - Partial or weak test · scott-dunphy/kryptos-k4, barneyjm/kryptos-k4, Greg (Reddit) (via TRIED.md compilation), by 2026-11 · [source](https://github.com/scott-dunphy/kryptos-k4)
- **Observation: the implied key looks random (high linear complexity)**
  - Scope: Linear complexity of crib-implied keys
  - Outcome: If a short key was used, it was expanded nonlinearly
  - Partial or weak test · barneyjm/kryptos-k4 (via TRIED.md compilation), by 2026-11 · [source](https://github.com/barneyjm/kryptos-k4)

## periodic-substitution

- **Periodic Vigenere, Beaufort or Variant Beaufort**
  - Scope: Periods 1-48; A-Z and KRYPTOS alphabets; both cribs (22-34, 64-74)
  - Outcome: Ruled out; periods 27-29 fit only because the cribs place no constraint there; planted-solution tests confirm detection
  - Solid elimination · gereon/kryptos, 2026-09 · [source](https://github.com/gereon/kryptos)
- **Porta, Playfair, or any cipher that cannot map a letter to itself**
  - Scope: Self-encryption K->K at position 74
  - Outcome: Ruled out by the K->K fixed point at position 74
  - Solid elimination · keithadler/kryptos, 2026-09-29 · [source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/keithadler/kryptos/main/SEARCH.md)
- **Repeating-key tableau cipher (Vigenere/Beaufort/Variant over 8 alphabet families)**
  - Scope: Key lengths 1-26, all 8 families; exact crib-constraint proof
  - Outcome: Ruled out at 1-26; lengths >=27 not constrained by the cribs; validated on 21 planted ciphers
  - Solid elimination · keithadler/kryptos, 2026-09-29 · [source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/keithadler/kryptos/main/SEARCH.md)
- **Progressive key K[i mod p] + s*floor(i/p)**
  - Scope: p <= 26, all 8 tableau families
  - Outcome: Ruled out; only one-constraint survivors at p=26, as chance predicts
  - Solid elimination · keithadler/kryptos, 2026-09-29 · [source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/keithadler/kryptos/main/SEARCH.md)
- **Digit keys: Gronsfeld, Gromark, or any 0-9 keystream**
  - Scope: A-Z, KRYPTOS and all 209k dictionary alphabets
  - Outcome: Ruled out: no alphabet makes all 24 shifts digits (best 21/24); does not cover arbitrary-alphabet Gromark
  - Solid elimination · keithadler/kryptos, 2026-09-29 · [source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/keithadler/kryptos/main/SEARCH.md)
- **Monoalphabetic substitution alone**
  - Scope: Crib proof: ciphertext F stands for E at 22 and for O at 72
  - Outcome: Ruled out by proof
  - Solid elimination · everyone (standard crib argument) (via TRIED.md compilation), by 2026-11 · [source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kryptos)
- **Key restarts at the start of both cribs**
  - Scope: Any key length, any alphabet; also shown by markmnl (https://github.com/markmnl/kryptos)
  - Outcome: Ruled out by proof: 3 letters in, T->V and L->V clash
  - Solid elimination · reggiechan74/kryptos-k4, markmnl/kryptos (via TRIED.md compilation), by 2026-11 · [source](https://github.com/reggiechan74/kryptos-k4)

## Open gaps (NOT negatives: untested or undecidable)

From `negatives/open_gaps.md`. This is where new ideas should aim.


These came out of the community research notes (2026-11-15). None of them has been ruled out. Each one is open because the cribs can't decide it, because nobody has searched it, or because a search was cut off before it finished.

### Periodic / keyed-alphabet
- **Arbitrary-alphabet periodic substitution at undecidable periods (8, 11-13, 16, 18-20, 22-24, 26+).** The 24 crib letters put no constraint on these periods. zeyeteam reports periods 19 and 38 as undecidable. Source: https://github.com/zeyeteam-debug/kryptos-k4-eliminations
- **Repeating keys longer than 26.** The cribs don't constrain lengths of 27 or more, and keithadler lists these as open. Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/keithadler/kryptos/main/SEARCH.md
  - *Partly closed 2026-11-15 by this repo (attack 06):* at key lengths 27, 28 and 29, the only short lengths the clues leave open, Vigenère/Beaufort/variant on A–Z, KRYPTOS and 208,648 dictionary-keyword alphabets show no signal. The 24 clue letters force 24 key letters, the 3–5 free ones were brute-forced or hill-climbed, and K4 scores the same as shuffled K4s. Still open: lengths 53+ and arbitrary (non-keyword) alphabets at 27–29. Source: experiments/ledger.csv (long-period-27-29)
- **Quagmire III, Beaufort form, arbitrary alphabet, at L=13 and L=18.** The exact solver timed out after 30 minutes. Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/keithadler/kryptos/main/SEARCH.md
- **Quagmire I/II at lengths 13, 16, 19, 20, 23, 24, 26, and Quagmire IV at 8, 13, 16, 19, 20, 23, 24, 26.** The cribs give at most 1 constraint at these lengths, or random texts pass just as often, so the result tells us nothing. Annealing could not detect two free alphabets at L=8, Quagmire II at L≥20, or Quagmire I at L=26. Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/keithadler/kryptos/main/SEARCH.md
- **Two free alphabets at once, and stacked layers with long keys.** keithadler states both are open. 472 two-layer key-length pairs have too many unknowns to solve. Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/keithadler/kryptos/main/SEARCH.md
- **Some keyword-mixed-alphabet variants.** kryptosbot says these are "not yet fully closed". Source: https://www.kryptosbot.com/

### Gromark / key generators
- **Gromark with arbitrary plain and cipher alphabets.** 39 base-10 primers with 5 digits survive the cribs, but they were only checked by annealing. Other bases, other primer lengths, and expansion rules that don't use the first primer digit are not closed. keithadler's digit-key result covers only A-Z, KRYPTOS and dictionary alphabets. Source: https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/153/109
- **Chaocipher, run dictionary by dictionary.** This was explicitly left untested. Source: https://github.com/Barneyjm/kryptos-k4
- **Subtractive feedback keys.** Only the short-primer bounded version was excluded. The subtractive feedback itself was not tested. Source: https://github.com/reggiechan74/kryptos-k4
- **Wichmann-Hill PRNG beyond seeds 0-50.** Only a tiny fraction of the roughly 30,000³ seed space was searched. Source: https://www.numberworld.blog/2017/03/kryptos-cipher-part-2.html

### Transposition (with or without substitution)
- **Myszkowski and disrupted columnar at width 10 or more, keyed routes, and irregular hand masks.** zeyeteam lists these as open. Source: https://github.com/zeyeteam-debug/kryptos-k4-eliminations
- **Columnar + periodic beyond the exhaustive range.** The search is exhaustive only to width 11. Widths 12-14 were pruned and bounded, and wider orders were tested only for keyword-derived orders. Source: https://github.com/Barneyjm/kryptos-k4
- **Keyed double columnar combined with substitution, route ciphers other than columns and rotations, and typo-tolerant transposition.** keithadler states these are open. Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/keithadler/kryptos/main/SEARCH.md
  - *Partly closed 2026-11-15 by this repo (attack 07):* double columnar with every column order at widths 2–7 on both layers (34.9M pairs), plus themed keyword orders up to width 15, with a periodic Vigenère or Beaufort key (A–Z or KRYPTOS alphabet, length 1–26) placed before, between or after the two layers. That is 11.1 billion hypotheses with 0 survivors (at least 6 clue equalities required). A planted test recovers a hidden stack exactly. Still open: widths 8+ with arbitrary orders, dictionary-keyword pairs, and scrambled (Quagmire) alphabets in the stack. Source: experiments/ledger.csv (double-columnar+periodic)
- **10×10 turning grille beyond the roughly 42% exactly searched (one variant only).** The annealer gave no clear answer. Source: https://github.com/zeyeteam-debug/kryptos-k4-eliminations

### Polygraphic / fractionation
- **Hill 6×6 and larger.** Searches covered only up to 5×5. Source: https://github.com/zeyeteam-debug/kryptos-k4-eliminations
- **Hill 4×4 at the two block alignments the cribs cannot test.** These are eliminated only under guessed cribs. Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/keithadler/kryptos/main/SEARCH.md
- **Fractionation on a 6×6 square.** keithadler lists it as open. A 6×6 Bifid alone was judged infeasible. Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/keithadler/kryptos/main/SEARCH.md

### Clock / physical / running key
- **The 1990 Weltzeituhr configuration: plate order, city names, and per-letter lookups from before 1997.** The 1990 plate order is unavailable, so the 1990 setup was only partly tested. Source: https://github.com/zeyeteam-debug/kryptos-k4-eliminations
- **Running keys from obscure texts.** Only obvious corpora were searched (K1-K3, Carter, Gutenberg-scale English and German, the KJV, US founding documents). An obscure source can't be ruled out. Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/keithadler/kryptos/main/SEARCH.md
- **Hand "masking", null or extra-character models, and "visual" or physical steps.** Scheidt says the masking technique "may not be known". Sanborn describes his own "visual systems", and no search has modelled them. Source: http://www.wired.com/news/culture/0,1284,66359,00.html

### Untested observations
- **The Stehle (2000) offset-letter-difference in DIAWINFBN.** Each of its first five letters minus the letter four places to its right gives 5 mod 26. Bean calls this unusual but did not test it. Source: https://ecp.ep.liu.se/index.php/histocrypt/article/download/153/109
- **A K4/K5 depth attack.** Sanborn says K5 shares coded words at the same positions as K4. The K5 ciphertext has not been released yet; Paradigm plans to release it. Source: https://www.paradigm.xyz/writing/kryptos

### Source-coverage gaps (research, not cipher space)
- **Reddit, groups.io and Yahoo archives.** None of them could be fetched (Reddit was blocked, groups.io returned HTTP 402), so posted forum scopes are missing. Source: https://groups.io/g/kryptos
- **The NSA 2007 summary and DOC_7.** Both returned 403, so their eliminations are not captured. Source: https://media.defense.gov/2021/Jul/12/2002760458/-1/-1/0/DOC_7.PDF
