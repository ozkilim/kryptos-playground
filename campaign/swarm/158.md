# Idea 158: a recited list as the key

**Verdict: dead in scope.** No list fits K4 on any of 666,993 keyword tableaux or on one hidden alphabet. 98.2% of
all list starts cannot fit on any tableau at all.

## The idea

Scheidt says the method is simple and can be run from memory years later. The longest keys people carry in their heads
are recited lists: ONE TWO THREE, boxing the compass (EASTNORTHEAST is a point of that list), the hours, the months,
the planets on top of the World Clock, EINS ZWEI DREI. No earlier run used a named list as an exact key.

## What was run

`attacks/158_recited_list_keys.py`, lists and tools in `kryptos/idea158_lists.py`, test in `tests/test_idea_158.py`.
The run took 7.4 minutes on 16 processes.

* **Keys:** 270 lists built in code. Number words and ordinals in English and German (0-120, rounds such as 1-12,
  1-24, 1-31, 1-60, evens, odds, fives, tens; up and down). The 32, 16, 8 and 4 compass points, both turning
  directions, long and short, English and German. Hours (ONEOCLOCK, EINUHR, AM/PM, military), Roman numerals,
  months, days, planets, zodiac, NATO / Able-Baker / Greek letter names, the alphabet, and the World Clock city ring
  facet by facet. German both as FUENF and FUNF. Each list forwards and letter-reversed: 740 key texts, used from
  every start letter: **387,800 starts**.
* **Gate (any tableau):** on a tableau with distinct rows and columns, R→P at letters 28 and 66 needs the same key
  letter at both, and 22 other clue pairs need different key letters.
* **Keyword tableaux:** 666,993 alphabets × 5 layouts × Vigenère / Beaufort / variant / Beaufort with negated key ×
  key letter valued on A-Z, KRYPTOS or the tableau's own alphabet × all 26 shifts. Exact: 24 of 24 or nothing
  (hash join, 4.0e14 settings).
* **One hidden alphabet:** idea 93's 25 models (numeric key, true Quagmire III and II, hidden alphabet on one side),
  solved mod 26 with the letters in different places.
* **Near misses:** most clue letters right at once on plain A-Z / KRYPTOS tableaux, any shift.

## Worth knowing

* **The boxed compass from ENE is impossible on every tableau.** It puts key letters T and S at letters 28 and 66,
  where R→P twice demands the same letter. No alphabet can repair that.
* From their own first letter, only 2 forward lists pass the gate: English numbers 60 down to 1 and German ordinals
  97 down to 1. Both then fail the tableau and hidden-alphabet tests.
* Lists with few different letters die completely at the gate: Roman numerals, the 4- and 8-point compass, the days,
  the alphabet itself (0 starts pass).
* The gate costs nothing and removes 98% of starts for any known key text. It was the only place where K4 differs
  from most shuffles, and that is just the known repeated R→P pair.

## Scope limits

* Only these 270 lists and these spellings, letters run together, one key letter per text letter, running on from the
  start letter. No restart per word or row, no skipping.
* The World Clock ring uses the partly reconstructed 1989 plates (17 of 24 facets not fully verified) and the
  post-1997 plates.
* Tableaux: the 666,993 keyword alphabets, or one fully hidden alphabet in idea 93's models. Two different hidden
  alphabets, a transposition, or a second layer are not covered. The 7,017 starts that pass the gate are closed only
  for these tableaux.
* Clue positions are taken as exact.
