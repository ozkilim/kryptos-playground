# Idea 96: the message enciphered back to front (autokey that looks ahead)

**Verdict: dead in scope.** No sign that K4 is an autokey whose key letters come from later in the text.

## The idea

Sanborn (2005): he left "instructions in the earlier text that refer to later text". If K4 was enciphered from its
last letter backwards, an autokey takes each key letter from a letter that FOLLOWS: `key[i] = word[i mod p] + sign *
number(letter at i + d)`, plaintext or ciphertext feed. At d = 42 (K3 worksheet width, the distance between the clues)
BERLINCLOCK would be the key for EASTNORTHEAST. Ideas 55, 59, 66, 74 and 90 all look back; idea 54 looked 1-5 ahead
on A-Z and KRYPTOS only. The equations are different from the look-back ones (another cipher letter belongs to each
pair), so this is a new test, not a relabelling.

## What was run

`attacks/96_enciphered_back_to_front.py` (helpers in `kryptos/idea96_ahead.py`), 12 minutes. All tests are exact on the
clue letters; a setting passes with 7 or more checks (pairs minus word length).

* **A, dictionary tableaux.** A-Z, KRYPTOS and every dictionary keyword alphabet in plain, `~cols` and `~keyed` form
  (666,993), five layouts, Vigenere and Beaufort, fed letter numbered in M, A-Z or KRYPTOS, added or subtracted, a
  constant or a word of 2-4 on top. Plain feed: d = 1-8 inside a clue (22 down to 8 pairs) and d = 34-48 with the second
  clue moved -3..3 (8-11 pairs; 11 at d = 42). Cipher feed: d = 1-68 (24 pairs up to d = 23, 13 for d = 34-63, 8 at
  d = 68). 139 (feed, d, shift) tests, **18.49 billion settings**.
* **B, mirror of idea 59.** A-Z / KRYPTOS on each side, any multiplier 1-25 on the fed letter, a word of 1-13 on top,
  same feeds and distances: 333,200 settings.
* **C, mirror of idea 55.** One hidden alphabet (both sides, or one side against A-Z / KRYPTOS):
  `x[C] = +-x[P] +- x[fed] + c` is linear in the 26 unknown places; solved mod 26, and a solution must put different
  letters in different places. d = 1-8, both feeds: 320 models; 3+ checks needed to count (as in idea 55).

## Pre-filter

FLAG, as expected: the K5 clue fails (a shared word encrypts the same way in 0% of message pairs, because another
message gives another key). Facts pass (S->S / K->K fixed points in 24% of probes, hand method). It explains the lag-4
run hint (x13 over chance). Reason to run anyway: exact, cheap (12 minutes), and the mirror image had never been run.

## Scope limits

* **Plain feed at d = 9-33 is not decided**: 5 or fewer checks at d = 9-12, no clue pairs at all at d = 13-33.
  Cipher feed at d = 69 and beyond has under 8 pairs.
* Part A: dictionary keyword alphabets only; multiplier +-1; words of 1-4 on top and only where 7+ checks remain
  (none at plain d = 8 or cipher d = 68). The second clue is moved only for plain feed d = 34-48.
* Part C cannot decide: plain feed d = 3-8 with both sides hidden, d = 7-8 with the plain side hidden, d = 8 with the
  cipher side hidden, and cipher feed d = 7 with both sides hidden. No word on top, no d beyond 8.
* No transposition, no mixed plain/cipher feed, no keyed (varying) distance ahead.

## Surprises

None for K4: not one pass in 18.49 billion settings, not even at the 7-check floor where a chance pass or two would
have been unremarkable (idea 90 had one).
The hidden-alphabet test is less sharp than the others: 3 of 20 shuffled K4s give 1-5 chance survivors at 3+ checks,
so a K4 survivor there would have needed more than the count to mean anything. K4 gave none.
