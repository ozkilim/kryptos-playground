# Idea 133: a small-shift key on the first part only (hidden plain alphabet against A-Z)

**Verdict: dead in scope.** Periodic plants recovered 3/3. Free-key plants are not read back, but they do stand out by score.

**The idea.** Idea 112 found one anomaly. With a hidden plain alphabet and cipher letters on A-Z, EASTNORTHEAST alone
fits a key window of 5, which only 0.3% of shuffled K4s do. Maybe letters 1..E (E before BERLINCLOCK) were enciphered
with small numbers (0-4 or 0-5) on a mixed alphabet, and the rest by another system. Model: C = AZ[x(P) + k],
k in 0..w-1. Code: `attacks/133_first_part_small_shift.py` and `kryptos/idea133_beam.py`.

**Exact facts.** The block allows 4,288 arrangements of E A S T N O R H at w=5 and 36,960 at w=6.

| Period | 2-6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 | 11 | 12 | 13 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| w=5 settings | 0 | 0 | 7 | 0 | 84 | 152 | 1,072 | 4,288 |
| w=6 settings | 0 | 4 | 18 | 0 | 344 | 932 | 7,392 | 36,960 |

So a short repeating digit key (period 2-6) is impossible here.

**Part B: periodic keys.** The 13 clue key values fix the whole key, so each reading is a simple substitution on the
empty places. Every allowed setting was hill-climbed for E = 49 and E = 63 (w=5 periods 2-13, w=6 periods 2-11).

- **K4, w=5:** best -4.613 (period 13: NCHSININLETBYTHREDFCW·EASTNORTHEAST·STIONTEGOTPLYQU). Not English.
  The matched controls give -5.03 to -4.59; K4 beats 9 of 10.
- **K4, w=6:** best -4.806. It beats 4 of the 6 controls that have any period key.
- **Planted texts:** they score -4.13 to -4.44 and read back 36/36 and 34/36. The third plant (period 7, w=6) comes in
  at rank 2: a sister setting that differs in one key value scores slightly higher (-4.444 against -4.490).

**Part A: free key.** Beam search, width 2000. The beam grows letters 21→1, then 35→63, and keeps the alphabet exactly
as letter domains plus a matching.

The model is too loose. On any ciphertext it fits text at -2.6 to -2.9 per quadgram, well above real English (-4.5).
The output is word salad from the pydoc-built model ("...DEFILE·EASTNORTHEAST·ATIONSASSPECIFI"). Planted texts are
not read back (7-14 of 36 letters).

As a score it still works. Each plant beats its own matched shuffles 20/20, 16/20 and 20/20. Against 100 matched
controls, K4 beats 45/100 at w=5 and 88/100 at w=6. That is inside the controls. With only 10 controls K4 looked
10/10 at w=6, which is why the 100 were run.

**Controls.** Shuffled K4s whose first block also fits w ≤ 5. They come up at 0.39% of seeds, the same rate as idea 112
found, so the controls carry the same anomaly as K4.

**Scope.**

- Covered: the ptX/AZ layout only, windows 5 and 6, regime ends E = 49-63.
- Not covered: wider windows (digits 0-9), periods 12-13 at w=6 (cut for time), transposition, and anything about the
  second part.
- Periodic readings come from a hill climb, not a proof of the best reading.

**Surprising.** Periods 2-6 and 9 are flatly impossible on any alphabet. Also, a free small-number key cannot be read
with quadgram completion at all: it needs a key rule from outside.
