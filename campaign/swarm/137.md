# Idea 137: Quagmire III Beaufort at key lengths 23 and 26, any alphabet, judged by English

**Verdict: no signal. The search is too weak to settle the question, so this is not a negative for the cipher.**

## The idea

Idea 130 closed Quagmire III in the Beaufort form (key = x[C] + x[P] on one hidden alphabet) for every alphabet at
every length 1-26 except 23 and 26. At those two lengths the clue equations can be satisfied, by K4 and by many
shuffles, so only an English score can judge them.

## What was run

`attacks/137_quagmire3_beaufort_23_26_climb.py`, with the search in `kryptos/idea137_anneal.py`. It runs
parallel tempering over full alphabets (8 temperatures) plus the key values of the residues with no clue letter
(6 at L=23, 3 at L=26). The clue equations are hard. After each swap, the letters the equations force are put back
on their forced places, and the move is dropped if an equation still fails. So every reading keeps all 24 clue
letters. Each text got 3 runs of 4M moves on the big quadgram model, then a greedy polish. In all, 360M decrypts in
29 minutes.

The Vigenere form needed no search. At L=23 the clues force x[P] = x[R], and at L=26 they force x[F] = x[E], so no
alphabet exists at either length.

## Numbers

| | K4 | 10 satisfiable shuffles | at or above K4 | planted (cold start) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L=23 | -5.212 | -5.429 to -5.130 (median -5.273) | 1/10 | 0/4 (found -5.00 to -5.10; truth -4.19 to -4.64) |
| L=26 | -5.279 | -5.350 to -4.967 (median -5.134) | 8/10 | 0/4 (found -4.90 to -5.12; truth -4.03 to -4.66) |

K4's best readings are gibberish: `BWHENSTOGCANTOODCWSAMEAST...` at L=23 and `QJKARLBECFCINTTBESMGLEAST...` at L=26.
Planted readings from a cold start got 32-48 of 97 letters right, and 24 of those are the clue letters.

The mechanics check passed 8/8. Started one swap from the planted alphabet with random free keys, the climber returns to
the truth, 97/97 letters. So decryption, the constraint handling and the scoring are right. Finding the truth is the
problem.

## Scope limits

* The search found 0 of 8 planted answers from a cold start, so this run cannot rule the cipher out at 23 or 26.
  Logged as `partial`.
* More compute did not help. A run at 7.5x the budget on an L=23 plant reached -5.06 against a truth of -4.58, the same as
  the normal budget.
* Only a plain repeating key at 23 or 26, the carved clue positions, and one alphabet on both sides.

## What was surprising

* The truth sits in a very small basin. Three random swaps away, the climber usually could not get back. Six swaps away,
  it never did. A swap that touches a clue letter changes the key of whole residue classes, so the landscape is very
  rugged.
* Decoys and shuffled controls reach -4.97 to -5.1 with all clue letters exact, which is at the usual "English" line.
  At these lengths a quadgram score of about -5.0 means nothing.
* An exact penalty-free walk was needed. A soft penalty on the 7 equations at L=23 almost never reached a state where all
  of them held.
