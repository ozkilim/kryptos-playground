# Idea 125: misspelled keywords and other ways to build the alphabet

**Verdict: dead in scope.** Nothing passes at 8+ checks. The only K4 pass is one coincidence at exactly 7 checks, which
chance predicts, and it cannot be read. The controls give nothing. All 6 planted answers were found.

## The idea

Sanborn misspells words: PALIMPCEST, IQLUSION, UNDERGRUUND, DESPARATLY. A keyword alphabet built from a misspelt
word is in no dictionary. Idea 86 solved any keyword exactly, but only for repeating and sliding keys. The other key
rules had only ever seen dictionary spellings, and only alphabets with the keyword first and the rest in A-Z order.

## What was run (`attacks/125_misspelled_keyword_alphabets.py`, helpers `kryptos/idea125_alphabets.py`, 45 min)

* **Part 1:** every single spelling change (replace any letter, drop one, double one, swap two neighbours) of
  KRYPTOS, the 85 theme words and the 21,425 common words. Each changed word gave its keyword alphabet and its two
  transposition mixes. After dropping the 666,993 dictionary alphabets and duplicates, **8,130,513** were new.
* **Part 2:** every one of the 236k vocabulary words, with the alphabet finished another way: the rest in reverse
  order, the rest wrapping on from the keyword's last letter, repeats kept at their last place, the keyword at the end,
  and the K1-K3 swaps Q/L, U/O, A/E, C/S applied to the keyword alphabet. **1,661,916** were new.
* **Battery:** idea 120's version (repeating 1-17, sliding 1-15, progressive 1-12, autokey lags 1-6, chain addition,
  every stacked pair exactly, and idea 104's autokey and progressive fixes). Five layouts, Vigenere and Beaufort,
  7+ exact checks. Chance is 3.3e-8 per alphabet, so 0.32 passes are expected over the 9.79M alphabets.
* **Join:** attack 63's exact hash join (Quagmire IV: plain alphabet, cipher alphabet, key length 1-20). The part-2
  alphabets were added to the dictionary list on both sides, giving 2.33M per side and 9.96e12 new pairs per length.

## Numbers

|---|---|---|
| battery passes, 7+ checks | 2 in 9.79M (chance 0.32) | 0 / 0 / 0 in 1.63M each (chance 0.05 each) |
| battery 8+ checks | 0 | 0 |
| join matches | 133 | 1,375 / 1,819 / 650 |
| join best reading (big model) | -7.24 | -7.21 / -7.11 / -7.44 |

The two K4 passes come from one alphabet, DISABLETFILL (a one-letter change of DISABLEDFILL), on AZ/M Beaufort,
with two stacked keys p=6 and q=13 or 39. The key lengths are nested, so it is one event. The clues fix too few
letters to read it (`?P??E??Q?M??O??Z...`).

The join counts per key length are uneven, for both K4 and the controls. That comes from repeated (plain, cipher)
pairs among the clue letters. For example, K4 positions 27 and 65 are both R->P and fall in the same slot at p=19,
which gives a free check: chance rises to about 47, and K4 had 52. At p=17 and 18 K4's clue letters contradict
themselves, so no alphabet pair fits at all.

**Planted: 6/6.**
* Battery plants: a sliding key of 7 on PALIMPCEST, a progressive key of 8 on DESPARATLY, plain autokey lag 5 on
  KRYPTOS with the reversed tail, a stacked pair 5+9 on TESTRESULTS with the wrap tail, and a repeating key of 17 on
  RFCREATING. Each was searched over its true alphabet plus 3,000 random ones. Only the true alphabet passed, and its
  setting with the most checks read back 97/97 (English -4.35 to -4.94).
* Join plant: searched over the full join, it ranked 1st of 32,019 matches (-4.58 against -7.16 for the next).

## Scope limits

* Part 1 covers only ONE spelling change, and only in theme and common words. Two or more changes, and misspellings
  of rarer words, are not covered.
* Part 2 covers exactly the constructions listed above, not combined with each other or with the transposition mixes.
* Idea 104's gap, modulus, multiplier, dropped-letter and word-break variants were not run.
* The join covers the part-2 alphabets only. Part 1 (8.1M) is too big for the in-memory join.
* The battery controls ran on 1/6 slices instead of the planned 1/3, to keep the run under an hour.

## Surprises

* The main run's pass/fail rule for planted answers was too strict, and marked 2 of 6 "NOT CLEAN". In the stacked
  plant, weaker settings on the true alphabet passed at 7-8 checks (nested key lengths) and filled in letters
  differently. In the join plant, tens of thousands of chance matches at 7 checks gave different readings. The rule
  was changed: the true setting must be among those with the most checks, or must read best by a clear margin. The
  plants were then rerun at full size with the same seed (`--plants-only`, log `/tmp/idea_125_plants.log`). The
  readings did not change.
