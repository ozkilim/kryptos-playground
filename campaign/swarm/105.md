## The surprises (each miss, smallest setting, exact fix)

1. **Attack 90, a real bug.** `_order()` in `kryptos/idea90_rowkey.py` splits runs on the clue index, not the
   position, so at cipher lags 30-33 the quick test compares letters 33 apart. Words of 2 and 4 fail at lags 30-33,
   words of 3 at 31-33 (13/13 missed; the repository's own `encrypt` is missed too). Smallest: lag 30, word of 2.
   Fix: `if pr[1] == cur[-1][1] + 1`. About 440 million of the 31.6 billion settings were never tested.
2. **Attack 59.** `if len(use) < 12: continue` skips cipher lags 34-60 (11 letters; key 1-5 gives 6-10 checks) and
   plain lags 7, 8. Smallest: cipher lag 34, key length 1. Fix: delete the line.
3. **Attack 88, progressive.** The battery takes the step from two clue letters p apart in one unbroken run. With
   EAST|NORTH|EAST + BERLIN|CLOCK lengths 6-12 are skipped (24/24 missed; 1-5 found 24/24); with EAST|NORTHEAST +
   BERLIN|CLOCK 9-12; with BERLINCLOCK unbroken 11-12. Fix: take the step from any two letters with the same
   place mod p in different passes (in `round11.battery` and in `idea88_slip._tables`).
4. **Stacked keys.** The pre-filter `c1 + c2 < 8` of idea 91's hole (a) repeats in 68 (123 of 242 missed), 70 (100
   of 205), 81 (77 of 114) and 88 (80 of 119; even 4,5 and 3,7). Fix as in idea 91: the exact solve.
5. **60C** needs every pair to agree; the ACA same-column rule strikes 1 pair in 26, so about 35% of real Slidefair
   messages are missed. Fix: allow C1 = P1 + 1, C2 = P2 + 1.
6. **61**: add the clockwise turn (the read order reversed). **69B**: join on key letters (26 offsets per row), or
   say "key as numbers". **57/80**: also read M[s + c] and KRYPTOS[s + c].

None of this says anything about K4. It says where K4 has not been tested.
