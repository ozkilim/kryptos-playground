# Idea 101: the second pass of the key word differs

**Verdict: dead in scope.** 0 of 28.6 billion exact tests pass on K4; the shuffled K4s give 0 too; 7 of 7 planted
messages were found.

## The idea

PALIMPSEST is a sheet written over twice, K2 ends LAYER TWO. Suppose the encoder changes something each time the key
word comes round: reads it backwards, switches Vigenere to Beaufort, moves it along, or switches chart. Then the key
repeats only after two passes. A plain repeating-key test at the word's length sees nothing, and the doubled length
(28-48) was outside every exact test so far. But the rule that links the two passes ties clue letters together, so
there are still 7 or more exact checks.

## What was run

`attacks/101_second_pass_differs.py` (helper `kryptos/idea101_passes.py`). On each of 666,993 keyword alphabets
(A-Z, KRYPTOS, dictionary, and the two transposition-mixed forms), five layouts, Vigenere and Beaufort views:

* **mirror**: key read forwards then backwards, period 16-52, *every* start letter and all three ways to turn round
  (ABCDDCBA, ABCDCB, ABCDDCB); also restarting at the carved rows; also one fold over the whole text (a long key
  written out and then back: 11 checks when the fold is at the middle letter).
* **slide**: the same for a strip moved on by the word.
* **period**: plain repeating key of length 18-52 (lengths 38-46 have 7-11 checks and had not been run on these tableaux).
* **alt**: same word, form alternates by pass (Vigenere/Beaufort, Vigenere/variant, Beaufort/variant,
  Beaufort/minus-Beaufort), word length 2-52, every start.
* **shift**: odd passes use the word moved by one free constant.
* **tab**: same word, tableau alternates by pass between the keyword tableau and A-Z or KRYPTOS (9 form pairings,
  key letter found on the tableau's own alphabet or by A-Z row label).

Each setting is a list of clue letters that must show the same key letter. Checks = letters tied minus key slots.
Settings that tie the clues identically are one test. 7+ checks count; 5-6 checks are a chance gauge.

## Numbers

| | tests at 7+ checks | pass at 7+ | gauge: pass at 5 / 6 checks (chance says) |
|---|---|---|---|
| K4, all 666,993 alphabets | 28,576,617,156 | **0** (chance 0.35) | 82 / 6 (112 / 5.6) |
| shuffled K4 seed 1, a third | about 9.5 billion | 0 | 44 / 1 (37 / 1.9) |
| shuffled K4 seed 2, a third | about 9.5 billion | 0 | 31 / 1 (37 / 1.9) |
| shuffled K4 seed 3, a third | about 9.5 billion | 0 | 74 / 1 (37 / 1.9) |

Nothing on K4 reached the bar, so there is nothing to decrypt or read.

Planted, 7/7 (each hidden among 16,676 alphabets): a 16-letter phrase read back and forth on a dictionary tableau
(period 32; 13 checks, 67 of 97 letters read back, English -4.91); an 11-letter word alternating Vigenere/Beaufort
on KRYPTOS (97 of 97, -4.17); a 9-letter word alternating a dictionary tableau and A-Z (97 of 97, -4.15); a strip
moved by a 10-letter mirrored word (97 of 97, -4.10); an 8-letter word with odd passes moved by 7 and row restart
(97 of 97, -4.39); a 13-letter mirrored word with row restart (70 of 97, -4.58); 49 random letters written out and
back (found with 11 checks; only 2 letters outside the clues are forced, so the exact test decides alone).

## Scope limits

* Tableau alphabets from the lists only; no hidden alphabet. Tableau alternation only against A-Z or KRYPTOS.
* Mirror: only (period, start) pairs that give 7+ checks are decided (1,790 distinct mirror tests per view); pairs with fewer checks, mostly at longer periods, are not.
  Sliding form: whole-text count only.
* The change happens every pass. A change every row, every word or every third pass, three kinds of pass, a second
  pass that is a different word, and transposition are not covered.

## Surprises

* The planted answer was not always the *single* survivor, as the plan asked. The same key also satisfies weaker
  settings (the A-Z passes of the tableau-alternating message look like a period-18 key on plain A-Z; a word of 8
  with shifted odd passes is also a key of 24 on the rows). The true setting always had the most checks and no wrong
  reading scored as well.
* Using every reflection point instead of a few fixed starts costs nothing and exposed a very natural case nobody
  had tested: a key that is a palindrome about the middle of K4 pairs 11 clue letters of EASTNORTHEAST with
  BERLINCLOCK. It fails on every tableau.
* Shuffle 3's gauge count (74) is twice chance: shuffles differ by a factor of two at 5 checks, which is why the
  bar is 7.
