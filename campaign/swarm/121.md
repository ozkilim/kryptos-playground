# Idea 121: any linear cipher-feedback rule, many lags at once, free coefficients

**Verdict: dead in scope.** K4 gives 0 passes in 268.3 million exact settings. The shuffled K4s also give 0.
All 6 planted rules were found and read back with no wrong letters.

## The idea

The simplest masks you can remember are running sums and differences: add each letter to the cipher letter
before it, or to the one on the left plus the one above it on a worksheet. These are cipher-feedback rules with
several lags at once. Earlier work tested one lag at a time (59, 74, 90, 117) or one cipher term (110 B). We know
all 97 cipher letters, so the rule

    nC(C[i]) = s nP(P[i]) + a1 nF(C[i-1]) + ... + a15 nF(C[i-15]) + c     (mod 26)

gives one linear equation at each of the 24 clue letters: 24 equations in 16 unknowns, so 8 exact checks, solved
mod 13 and mod 2. The rule never has to be named. Order 15 includes every lower order: running sums of any order,
binomial masks, left-plus-above up to width 15 and any mix of lags 1-15. The pre-filter gives FLAG, because of the
K5 "same words in the same places" hint. Our written reason is that K5 may share only the words, not their cipher
letters.

## What was run

`attacks/121_cipher_feedback_any_lags.py` (helpers in `kryptos/idea121_feedback.py`, test in
`tests/test_idea_121.py`). Plain, cipher and fed letters are each numbered on the tableau alphabet M, A-Z or
KRYPTOS, with s = +1 or -1. The alphabets are 666,993 dictionary keyword alphabets with their two
transposition-mixed forms, plus A-Z and KRYPTOS.

* **A**: lags 1-15 plus a constant, 8 checks, on all alphabets. Gauge: lags 1-19 at 4 checks.
* **B**: 126 worksheet shapes {1,w}, {w-1,w,w+1}, {1,w,w+1} and {w,2w} for w up to 48, each with a lag over 15,
  with 7-21 checks. Shapes whose lags are all 15 or under are already inside A, and A is the looser test.
* **C**: plain feedback added: cipher lags 1-R plus plain lags 1-S, with (S,R) = (1,7), (2,6), (3,5), (4,4) and
  (5,1), giving 7-13 checks. Equations whose plain letters fall outside a clue block are dropped.
* **D**: cipher plus plain feedback with R+S = 6, plus a repeating word of 3, 4 or 5 instead of the constant
  (7-15 checks).
* B-D cost about 40 times as much per alphabet as A. They ran on the 254 theme alphabets and every 15th
  dictionary alphabet (44,450).

(the primer).

## Scope limits

* Keyword tableaux from the dictionary only (plus A-Z and KRYPTOS). No hidden alphabet.
* Linear mod 26, with only +P or -P on the plain letter. No other multiplier, no drift, no word longer than 5.
* Order 16 and up is decided only for the sparse shapes in B.
* Non-linear feedback (a table of the fed letter) is not covered; idea 117 handles single lags.
* B-D ran on the theme alphabets plus a 1-in-15 dictionary sample only.
* The rule must run unbroken through the clue letters at the carved positions.

## Surprises

None. One thing worth noting: in the plain-feedback models one plant had two solutions mod 26 that agree on
every letter after the clues but not before them. Survivors are therefore ranked on the whole text, with unread
letters counted as noise.
