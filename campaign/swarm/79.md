# Idea 79: gap slack for formula mining and the three-keyword join

**Verdict: dead in scope.** Nothing on K4 that the shuffled controls do not also give.

## The idea

Sanborn dropped a letter when cutting K2. If a letter or two is missing or extra between EASTNORTHEAST (21-33)
and BERLINCLOCK (63-73), every test that ties the clues together through their positions fails on the true
answer. Idea 68 checked this for the key-rule battery. Two other position-bound tests had only been run with the
carved gap: formula mining (54) and the Quagmire IV hash join (63).

## What was run

`attacks/79_gap_slack_formulas_and_join.py`, helpers in `kryptos/idea79_gap.py`. For each shift g in
-3, -2, -1, +1, +2, +3 the text is put back as the encipherer had it (g unknown letters re-inserted, or -g
letters removed, at position 48), which moves the second clue to 63+g..73+g. Attacks 54 and 63 then run unchanged.
Neither reads a ciphertext letter more than 5 places from a clue, so any slip between observed positions 39 and 57
gives the same result.

* **Formulas:** C = a*P + up to 3 of 186 ingredients + c (mod 26), A-Z and KRYPTOS alphabets, 8 or more checks.
  Sizes 0-3 only.
* **Join:** 666,993 dictionary keyword alphabets on each side (plain and two transposition mixes), Vigenere and
  Beaufort, key lengths 1-20. Every match decrypted and scored, and its key tested as a dictionary word. The 39-57
  stretch is left out of the decrypts because the place of the slip is unknown there.

Full run: 27 minutes.

## Numbers

| | K4 | shuffled 1 | shuffled 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Formula fits (8+ checks), all six shifts | 0 of 25,743,168 | 0 | 0 |
| Join: consistent settings, all six shifts | 13,091 | 12,254 | 452 |
| Join: best partial decrypt (quadgram mean) | -5.95 | -5.97 | -5.96 |
| Join: keys that can be a dictionary word | 0 | 0 | 0 |

The join matches all sit at key lengths 15-20, where a shifted second clue leaves only 5-9 independent checks
against 890 billion alphabet pairs, so thousands of chance matches are expected. The counts jump around
(4,843 at one length, 5 at another) for K4 and controls alike. K4's best decrypts are gibberish, for example
`D.NCDHEDELINGSDIXOTC.EASTNORTHEAST...` (gap +3, key length 19, DUCKING / KAKARALI~keyed).

**Planted: 6/6.** Three formula-key messages (column + row + previous plaintext) and three Quagmire IV messages
(key lengths 11-13), with 1 letter lost, 2 lost, or 1 added between the clues at a random place. Each was found at
its true shift only, and not with the carved gap, which shows the slack matters and the cut position does not.

## Scope limits

* Shifts of 1-3 only, slip assumed between observed positions 39 and 57.
* Formulas: no size 4; every ingredient (the carved-layout ones included) is taken at the shifted position;
  planted formulas were searched on the small library (grid widths 7 and 31).
* Join: key lengths 21-24 not run. At lengths 17-20 the matches are too many and too weakly checked to exclude
  one by one; they were judged by decrypt score and the word-key test only.
* 2 controls per shift.
* The clue pre-filter was not re-run: both families were pre-filtered for ideas 54 and 63 and the cipher is the same.

## Surprises

None in the results. One thing worth knowing: with a shifted gap the two clues overlap less in key slots, so the
join is much weaker than with the carved gap (as few as 5 checks at length 19 with gap +3).
