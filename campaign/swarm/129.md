# Idea 129: a repeating key word moved on by a steady step, on one hidden alphabet

**Verdict: dead in scope. Seven (layout, word length) cells are undecided.**

**The idea.** A person keeps a short key word and adds a fixed step, either at every letter or once per pass
through the word, on a tableau whose alphabet is anything at all:
k_i = w[i mod p] + s·i, or w[i mod p] + s·floor((i+phase)/p).

**What was run** (`attacks/129_stepped_word_hidden_alphabet.py`, helpers in `kryptos/idea129_step.py`). The
setup was p = 1-13 and s = 1-25, in both forms and at every start phase, over six layouts:
- one hidden alphabet on both sides, in Vigenere form (V) and Beaufort form (B);
- the plain side hidden against A-Z or KRYPTOS (hp);
- the cipher side hidden against A-Z or KRYPTOS (hc).

That is 15,450 settings. For a fixed setting the 24 clue letters give equations that are linear in the 26 places
and the p word values. These were solved exactly mod 13 and mod 2. Then an exact backtracking search had to find
a solution with every clue letter on a different place. Fits that could be listed in full were completed on the
big English model. The run took 44 minutes, so it went past the 30-minute target.

**What the clues can decide.** In V and B the word values absorb almost every loop. There are 3 loop tests at
p = 1, 2 at p = 2, 1 at p = 3-5 and none from p = 6. The half-hidden layouts keep about 12 − p tests, and from
p = 10 they have 2 or fewer.

**K4.**
- Half-hidden, p 1-9 (3-11 tests): 0 settings pass. Chance expects about 0.01.
- V and B, p = 1: 0 settings pass.
- V at p 2-7 and 9, and B at p 2-10: no placing puts the clue letters on different places, for any step. This is
  exact even with 0 loop tests.
- 332 settings had their fits listed in full (V p 8, hpAZ p 13, hcKR p 12) and were completed. The best reading
  is −6.04 (hcKR p 12, s 12 per letter: `KWMYITASQUEGSGRAPACFMEASTNORTHEAST...`). It is noise; English needs −5.1.

**Controls.** 20 shuffled K4s were run through the same search. Their best readings were −6.00 to −6.63, so K4's
−6.04 sits inside the spread.

**Planted.** 5 of 5 plants in decided cells came back as the best reading of their whole cell, at 92-97 of 97
letters and −4.3 to −4.8:
- V, word 7, step 3 per pass;
- hcKR, word 5, step 2 per pass;
- hpKR, word 5, step 2 per pass;
- V, word 1, step 7;
- hpAZ, word 9, step 4.

The idea's Beaufort word-11 plant (step 1 per letter) falls in an undecided cell. Its true setting survives the
exact test, but it could not be read back.

**Scope limits (not ruled out).**
- V with a word of 10-13 and B with a word of 11-13: there are 0 loop tests and fits in almost every setting,
  millions per setting.
- The hidden cipher side with a word of 13 (65 settings).

Two hill-climbs failed even on the planted text in these cells: one over the fit lattice, one over the whole
alphabet with the clues as penalties. Deciding these cells needs a real solver. Also not covered: words over 13,
steps that change, two different hidden alphabets, and a transposition layer.

**Surprising.** K4 is blocked by the different-places rule more often than shuffled K4s are. The shuffles have
fits at V p 7-9 (26-84 settings on average) and at B p 6-10 (4-198 on average); K4 has none. The plain repeating
key (s = 0) also has no distinct placing on K4 at any p from 1 to 12 in V and B, which matches the older
community result.
