# Idea 95: two rotors, one wired from a keyword and one hidden

**The idea.** Idea 82 tried two rotors in series with both wirings hidden and found that 24 clue letters cannot
judge 52 unknown contacts. Here one rotor is a strip anyone could rebuild from a keyword, and only the other is
secret: `Y = R1[P+s] - s`, then `C = R2[Y+t] - t`, with a fast turn `s` and a slow turn `t`. With one wiring known,
the middle letter `Y` is known at every clue letter. Each clue letter then gives one wire of the hidden rotor, and a
real wiring never sends one contact to two places or two contacts to one place (idea 72's test). The clue pre-filter
passes the model (PASS, score 1.0).

**What was run** (`attacks/95_keyword_rotor_plus_hidden_rotor.py`, helper `kryptos/idea95_rotor.py`).

* Form A: hidden rotor first, keyword rotor second. Form B: keyword rotor first, hidden rotor second.
* Keyword rotor: KRYPTOS, 84 theme words and 220,232 dictionary keyword alphabets, read as a strip against A-Z both
  ways round. For KRYPTOS and the theme words also the strip against itself, 1-25 places on.
* Fast turn: 26 speeds, previous cipher letter, running sum of the cipher text, every key of length 1-3, 85 theme
  words plain and sliding. Slow turn: block 2 shifted 0-25, or one step per 26 letters at every offset.
* Faces numbered A-Z or KRYPTOS. A setting counts with 7+ forced coincidences; those with 5-6 are also kept. Every
  survivor is completed (the forced part of the hidden wiring), all 97 letters are decrypted and scored.
* No triple loop: the rules are looked up through forced pairs of wires, so 3.6e12 settings took 20 minutes in all.

**Numbers.**

| | K4 | shuffled K4 seeds 1, 2, 3 |
|---|---|---|
| settings judged | 3,641,182,057,344 | about 1.2e12 each (a third of the dictionary each) |
| real wiring with 7+ coincidences | 0 | 0, 0, 0 |
| real wiring with 5-6 coincidences | 1 | 0, 0, 0 |
| best reading (big model; English needs -5.1) | -6.45 | nothing to read |

K4's one pass at 5 coincidences (form A, strip AZTHIONIUM, fast word NOVEMBER, KRYPTOS-numbered faces) reads
`?BD?ST?LKAKAKKFDIQ??OEASTNORTHEAST EGO?NKNNWILOSOXL...`: gibberish, word coverage 0.35. About one chance pass at 5
is expected in a search this size, so this is noise, not a lead.

Planted messages: **6/6** found and read back (both forms; constant speed, 3-letter key, theme word; 58-75 of 97
letters read, English score -3.9 to -4.6). Planted searches used the true strip, every 60th dictionary strip and
10 theme strips.

Power, from 1,000 random true settings each with K4's own clue words: 68% (form A, speed), 79% (form A, word),
85-90% (form B, speed and word) and 99-100% (3-letter keys) reach 7 coincidences; 99-100% of all kinds reach 5.
Since the run also kept and read everything with 5-6, very little of the scope could hide.

**Verdict: dead in scope.** K4 looks like the shuffled K4s. Nothing was found.

**Scope limits.**

* Dictionary strips only, against A-Z; the strip against itself only for KRYPTOS and theme words; no
  transposition-mixed alphabets.
* Fast keys longer than 3 only if they are theme words. Long turning keys that are not words stay open.
* The fast turn is always on the first rotor; both rotors turn the same way.
* In form A, a slow step inside a clue block was tried only on KRYPTOS and theme strips, and not for the 9-34 rules
  (of 1,068) that have no forced pair in the other block.
* Two hidden wirings (idea 82 form H), three rotors and a reflector are not touched.

**Surprises.**

* Unlike idea 82, this form is very decidable: on K4 93% of fast rules (form A) and 46 of 48 slow shapes (form B)
  reach 7 coincidences, and a wrong setting passes with chance near 1e-12.
* A planted short key drags sister settings along: for the form A 3-letter key 1,078 settings passed and one sister
  read slightly better than the planted setting. The test fixes the strip and the key length better than the exact key.
* Planted texts run several times slower than K4, because plain letters other than EASTNORTHEAST/BERLINCLOCK give
  the short-key rules fewer forced pairs inside a block.
