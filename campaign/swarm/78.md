# Idea 78: the round 11 battery on transposition-mixed keyword alphabets

**The idea.** Ideas 68, 70, 71 (part B) and 74 tried every plain keyword alphabet (220,318 of them). A keyword
alphabet is often mixed one step further by hand: write it in rows under the keyword and read it off by columns,
left to right (`~cols`) or in the alphabetical order of the keyword's letters (`~keyed`). Round 10 used those forms;
these four round 11 tests did not. This closes that gap.

**What was run.** `attacks/78_mixed_alphabet_battery.py` loads the four original attacks and calls their own test
functions unchanged (through `kryptos/idea78_mixed.py`), on every `~cols` / `~keyed` alphabet that is not also a plain
keyword alphabet: 446,675 alphabets (223,962 `~cols`, 222,713 `~keyed`), the full set, not only theme and common words.

* 68: each clue alone, and both clues with the gap off by -3..+3, battery of key rules
* 70: key arithmetic mod 27..31, same battery; multiplying key mod 29 and 31
* 71B: fixed multiplier 3..23 on the plaintext letter, then the battery
* 74: autokey where a repeating pattern says how far back to look (M/M tableau)

Every test is exact on the 24 clue letters and needs 7 or more independent checks.

Total 1,289,104,050 settings, 47 minutes. Planted messages, each built on a mixed alphabet with the original attack's
planting function, recovered 7/7: a sliding key with a dropped cipher letter (68), a key that changes word half way
(68), sliding keys mod 27 and mod 31 (70), multiplier 17 with a sliding key (71B), keyed-lag autokey with cipher feed
and with plain feed (74).

**Verdict: dead.** K4 gives nothing, exactly like the shuffled K4s. Not solved, no signal.

**Scope limits.**

* To keep the run short, each shuffled K4 was tested on a different third of the alphabets (every third one, about
  148,900 each), and each planted message on a sample of 9,001 alphabets that includes its own. K4 was tested on all.
* Only the two mixing methods in `kryptos.round11.mixes`, on dictionary words of 3-22 letters. Other ways of mixing
  an alphabet, and keys that are not words, are not covered.
* Part A of attack 71 and the A-Z/KRYPTOS views of attack 74 do not use dictionary alphabets, so they were not re-run.
* Each test keeps the limits of its original attack (periods, lags, gap range -3..+3, 74 on M/M only).

**Surprises.** None. The run took 47 minutes rather than the planned 30 because the machine was shared.
