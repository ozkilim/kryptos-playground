# Idea 118: close the holes the idea-105 audit found

**Verdict: dead-in-scope.** K4 has 0 passes in every hole, and so do the shuffled controls. Every planted answer that has enough checks to judge is found.

## The idea

Idea 105 fed textbook-enciphered messages to twenty attacks. In seven of them it found settings that the negatives
say were tested but where K4 never really was. Idea 104 had closed only the stacked keys of 68, 70, 71B and 81.
This idea closes the rest. The old attacks are left untouched. Fixed copies of their test functions are in
`kryptos/idea118_holes.py`, and K4 is run only on the settings the old code missed.

  3,000 alphabets). Control: 52 messages with the clue one letter late gave 0 found.

## Scope limits

* Only the listed holes, each in its attack's own scope (alphabets, layouts and lengths as in the table). 59, 60C
  and 61 cover A-Z and KRYPTOS only. 69B tests only the two clue rows.
* Attack 88's two stacked keys were not rerun on K4. Idea 104's part "breaks" already ran the exact loop solve on all
  156 of 88's position sets; here that was only checked on the plants (39/39).
* Still open: the unaudited attacks 55, 58, 67, 75-77, 82, 83 and 85. Also untested: attack 81's progressive key at
  lengths 11-12 with the wrong letter inside clue 1, and 59 plain feed 9, 62 widths 10+, 65 taps 9-10 and 69A
  shifts -5/-4, none of which leave enough checks to judge.

## Surprises

* Attack 90's hole is exactly the 11 (lag, word) settings idea 105 predicted, about 440 million settings. Word 1 at
  lags 30-33 had been tested correctly all along.
* The 88 plants ignore the audit's "one letter late" offset, because they are built from spaced words. For that
  control the test was given the cipher letters moved one place instead.
* The 69B letter join is the only part where chance predicts close to one pass on K4 (0.71, with 9 checks). K4 gave 0.
