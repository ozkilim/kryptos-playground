# Idea 132: two wrong clue letters

**Verdict: dead-in-scope.** K4 has 0 passes in every test, with any two clue letters dropped.

## The idea

K1-K3 have at least five errors in them, some of them deliberate. Idea 81 allowed for one wrong
clue letter out of 24 and found nothing. With two wrong letters, every exact test so far fails on
the true method, idea 81's included. So here two letters are dropped:

* one from EASTNORTHEAST and one from BERLINCLOCK: 143 pairs, bar 7 checks;
* or both from one block: 133 pairs, bar 9 checks.

## What was run

`attacks/132_two_wrong_clue_letters.py` and `kryptos/idea132_droptwo.py`. Each rule of the
battery is written as a list of items that must agree within a class. For each rule the search
finds the exact sets of at most two clue letters whose removal clears every clash, by branching on
the first clash. It does not re-run the battery 276 times.

* **Battery with idea 104's hole fixes:** repeating to 17, sliding to 15, progressive to 12 (also
  moved on along A-Z, KRYPTOS or M with 26 offsets), autokey lags 1-6 with the fed letter numbered
  on M, A-Z or KRYPTOS, chain addition, and two stacked keys (exact loop test, q up to 26). All 5
  layouts, Vigenere and Beaufort.
  * Core list (A-Z, KRYPTOS, theme words, common words; 19,622 alphabets): all rules at bar 7.
  * The other 647,371 alphabets of the full 666,993 list: bar 7 would expect 1.30 chance passes,
    so the bar was raised to 8 (chance 0.053). The progressive hole form was left out there for
    time.
* **71A multiplier word + adder word:** 101,556 systems.
* **72 word-turned rotor:** 248,495 words, all schemes.

## Scope limits

* Exactly two wrong letters. Three or more are not covered.
* No hidden-alphabet tests, joins, formula mining, keyed-lag autokey, evolving alphabets, mod
  27-31, or gap and word-break variants.
* The progressive hole form ran on the core list only. The wide list ran at bar 8.
* Pairs with both letters in one block were judged only at 9+ checks.
* Sliding and autokey lose the pairs that run through a dropped letter.

## Surprises

* A wrong letter is not always visible. For cipher autokey lag 3, no check uses clue letters 21-23
  or 63-65. For a rotor, a wrong letter can land on an unused wire and still fit. In both cases no
  test can see the error. The plants were redrawn so that both changes are detectable.
* On a planted message, sister settings also pass on the true alphabet. Examples are progressive
  p=10 with other drop pairs, and stacked p=4 with q=14 or 21. On K4 and the controls nothing
  passes at all.
* Raising the bar is cheap here, and needed. Stacked keys carry most of the chance at bar 7:
  1.45e-7 of the 2.0e-7 per key stream.
