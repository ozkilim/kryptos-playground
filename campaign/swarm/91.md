# Idea 91: independent-encryptor audit of attacks 63, 66, 71, 72, 74, 78

**Verdict: no-signal** (an audit of the method; it makes no claim about K4).

## The idea

A search that cannot find a planted answer proves nothing. Every planted answer so far was made with the attack's
own helper code. A mistake shared by the planting code and the test (clue positions off by one, an alphabet indexed
the wrong way, a Beaufort sign) would pass its planted test and still miss the real K4.

## What was run

`kryptos/idea91_textbook.py` holds fresh encryptors written from the hand-cipher definitions (strips that slide, a key
letter set under an index letter, a rotor that turns). It imports nothing from the repository; K4, the clue positions
and the carved rows are typed in by hand. `attacks/91_independent_encryptor_audit.py` then does four things (21 s).

1. **Basics, 9/9.** K4 in `data/` equals the hand-typed K4; the carved rows are 4+31+31+31; letters 22-34 and 64-74 are
   FLRVQQPRNGKSS and NYPVTTMZFPK; `kryptos.CRIBS` and the position lists of rounds 9 and 11 are the same 24 positions.
2. **Sculpture, 7/7.** The strip code turns the K1 and K2 plaintexts into the carved ciphertext with PALIMPSEST and
   ABSCISSA (K2 both as carved and with the S restored), and rebuilds the 26 carved tableau rows.
3. **678 K4-shaped messages** (97 letters of English, EASTNORTHEAST at 22-34, BERLINCLOCK at 64-74), 6 for each of 113
   models with random in-scope settings, each given to the matching attack's own test function with the repository's
   CRIBS and 40 decoy alphabets.
4. **A map of two stacked keys**: every pair of lengths p < q <= 13, two messages each.

## The surprise: two scope holes in attack 66 (and in `kryptos.round11.battery`)

**(a) Two stacked keys: 20 of 41 length pairs are never tested.** The docstring says "lengths p < q <= 13". 41 pairs
have 7 or more exact checks. A quick pre-filter (`c1 + c2 < MIN_CHECKS + 1`) adds two partial counts that are not the
number of exact checks, and drops 20 of them before the exact solver runs:
(3,11) (3,13) (4,11) (4,13) (5,9) (5,11) (5,12) (5,13) (6,9) (6,10) (6,11) (6,13) (7,8) (7,9) (7,10) (7,11) (8,9) (8,10)
(8,12) (9,12). Attack 66's own exact solver accepts the true key at all 20. `round11.battery` (attacks 68, 70, 71B, 78,
81, 88) drops the same 20. Found pairs: (2,3) (2,5) (2,7) (2,9) (2,11) (2,13) (3,4) (3,5) (3,7) (3,8) (3,10) (4,5) (4,6)
(4,7) (4,9) (4,10) (5,6) (5,7) (5,8) (6,7) (6,8). 25 of the 60 stacked messages were missed. The attack's own planted
test used only (p, p+3) with p = 4-6, all in the found set, which is why it never showed.

**(b) Autokey only when the key letter is numbered on the keyword alphabet M.** 90 of 90 missed:

* the usual Quagmire I autokey: key letter found on the cipher strip, layouts M/AZ and M/KR (48/48 missed);
* the mirror convention (key letter found on the plain strip) on AZ/M and KR/M (24/24 missed);
* rows picked by their A-Z label, as the carved tableau is labelled, on M/M (12/12 missed);
* a progressive key moved on in A-Z order instead of along the mixed strip, on M/M (6/6 missed).

The attack's docstring does say "position taken in M", so this is a narrow scope rather than a bug. But the negative
reads as "autokey on every dictionary tableau in five layouts", which is more than was tested.

Neither hole says anything about K4. They say K4 has not been tested there on dictionary tableaux.

## Scope limits

* Only the test functions of attacks 63, 66, 71, 72, 74 and 78 (tests 68 clue-1-alone, 71B, 74). Not test 70, not the
  gap-slack part of 68, not attacks 54-62, 67, 69, 73, 75-77, 79-90, and not the English-scoring stage of any attack.
* 6 messages per model. Each true alphabet was handed over with 40 decoys, not inside the full 667,000-alphabet list;
  the planted alphabets were checked to be in each attack's own list (0 missing).
* Alphabets come from 46 audit words (one, DESPERATLY, is not in the repository's vocabulary; its alphabet is, through
  DESPERATELY).
* The audit shows the tests agree with the textbook definitions and with K1/K2 as carved. It cannot show that K4 uses
  any of these ciphers.

## Follow-ups

1. Replace the stacked-key pre-filter by the exact solve and rerun K4 and controls at the 20 dropped pairs.
2. Autokey with the feed letter numbered on A-Z, KRYPTOS or the other side's alphabet, on every dictionary tableau.
3. Extend the audit to the attacks it did not reach.
