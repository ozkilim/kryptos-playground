## What five rounds now say

* **No method for K4 has been found**, across ~40 of our own experiments plus 303 sourced community negatives.
* **Every evidence-led family we could formalise is closed or measured-blind**, including the newest clues (World Clock 1989, Egypt, LAYER TWO, misspellings, strips, teleprinters).
* **K4's statistics have no exploitable key-shape signature.** The mod-7 doublets and the lag-4 run hold up on their own, but they don't show up as periodic structure in the letter differences, and the key isn't smooth.
* The remaining space is mostly (1) things 97 letters + 24 clues can't decide (long stacked keys, long hidden-alphabet keys), and (2) Sanborn's non-mathematical "visual" or physical steps that we haven't yet found a way to write down.
